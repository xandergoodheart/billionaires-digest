1 Scoreboard strip (already in v3): live leaders across the top of every game page.
2 Hero banner: realistic art (no people), bold line. Swappable per week.
3 Your franchise card: your saved team's real points this week; empty state asks you to draft.
4 From the Digest: the day's real stories that involve players, linked to the edition.
5 New filter: Billionaires / CEOs / All. CEOs get a CEO badge everywhere.
6 Player rows gain a nickname line ("The Leather Jacket"). Nicknames are playful labels we write, never invented facts.
7 Player dossier: one pop-up used on every page (Draft room, Players, My team, Scores).
8 Tale of the tape: role start year, sector, market cap, employees. Each item sourced and dated.
9 Game log: shows the math. Stock return → base points (1% = 10 pts) → captain 1.5×, then day by day.
10 Company stats: real numbers from the latest SEC 10-Q/10-K with their dates. No made-up figures.
11 Scouting report: their latest filings, Digest stories, Billionaire Life game and Next Moves markets.
12 Score card: points this week vs the S&P 500, with a day-by-day bar chart.
13 Play the week: replay the real days one at a time (fun reveal, not a forecast).
14 Benchmark: your team vs the market, link to the leaderboard.
