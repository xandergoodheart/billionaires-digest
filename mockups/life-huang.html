About this prototype
  • Every date and number comes from a linked source: company filings, NVIDIA's own releases, a US regulator, a university, or news outlets.
  • Situations and outcomes are written in our own words and may be simplified.
  • The other choices on each card are made-up alternatives for the game. They are not claims about what he considered.
  • Scoring only checks whether you picked what he picked. It does not judge whether his choice was right.
