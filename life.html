    About Billionaire Life
    • Every date and number comes from a linked source: company filings and releases, regulators, universities or news outlets.

    • The other choices on each card are made-up alternatives for the game. They are not claims about what the person considered.
    • Scoring only checks whether you picked what they picked. It does not judge whether their choice was right.
