Meta stock jumps 11% in a day on Muse AI buzz

The move

Meta shares rose 11% in a single day and closed near a 52-week high after its Muse AI assistant, launched September 8, topped app-store charts with more than 2.5 million downloads. The move added over $100 billion to Meta's market cap, pushing its value close to $1.9 trillion.

Why it matters

Muse becoming a hit app is the market's first real evidence that Meta's massive AI spending can show up in a product people actually use, not just in ad-targeting algorithms.

The bear case

Most Muse features are still free, and it's unproven whether app-store buzz and downloads convert into paying subscribers at Meta's $20 or $100 monthly tiers.

What to watch

Whether Muse subscription uptake shows up in Meta's next earnings report.

Zuckerberg turns Meta's AI agent into a toll booth

The move

At Meta Connect's Sept. 23-24 sessions, Zuckerberg said Muse, Meta's AI agent, will stay free for a large number of tokens, then earn Meta a small fee on transactions it completes. Meta added PayPal support and shopping integrations with Walmart, Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, Wayfair and American Eagle, plus travel booking through Expedia and grocery orders via Instacart.

Why it matters

Meta is betting that owning the AI agent people shop through is worth more than ad revenue alone. The fee comes from merchants, not users, so wider Muse adoption compounds directly into Meta's revenue.

The bear case

The model depends on people trusting an AI agent to spend money on their behalf, and Meta hasn't said what the fee will be or when it starts.

What to watch

How many retailers and users actually adopt Muse checkout once it leaves the keynote stage.
