Berkshire nearly doubles its stake in homebuilder Lennar

The move

Berkshire Hathaway disclosed Sept. 26 that it now owns 25.9 million Lennar shares worth about $2.1 billion, up from 13.4 million shares as of June 30, after buying more than $348 million worth between Sept. 17 and Sept. 21.

Why it matters

Lennar's stock has slumped this year on a weak housing market, and Berkshire adding aggressively into that weakness is a classic bet that the market has overcorrected on homebuilders.

The bear case

At roughly $2.1 billion, the position is still small next to Berkshire's cash pile, and it's reportedly the work of portfolio manager Ted Weschler rather than Buffett himself.

What to watch

Whether Berkshire keeps adding to Lennar, and whether the homebuilder's results confirm a housing-market bottom.

Berkshire nearly doubles its stake in homebuilder Lennar

The move

Berkshire Hathaway disclosed Friday that it now owns about 25.9 million Lennar shares, worth roughly $2.1 billion and equal to 10.9% of the homebuilder, after buying roughly $349 million more stock between Sept. 17 and Sept. 26. That's nearly double the 13.4 million shares Berkshire held as of June 30, and the purchase pushed Berkshire's stake past the 10% threshold that triggers mandatory SEC disclosure. Lennar shares were down 20.1% for the year and had fallen 9.2% for the week when Berkshire kept buying.

Why it matters

Berkshire is betting on a longer-term recovery in the struggling U.S. housing market, buying a homebuilder while its stock is beaten down rather than waiting for a turnaround to become obvious.

The bear case

Even after nearly doubling, the $2.1 billion Lennar position is still a small stake for a company Berkshire's size, and homebuilder stocks could stay weak if mortgage rates don't come down.

What to watch

Whether Berkshire keeps adding to the Lennar stake, and any change in its size in Berkshire's next quarterly 13F filing.

Warren BuffettInvestmentReal estateBRK.A · LENAI read · ▲ Bullish · buying the dip

Berkshire builds a nearly 10% stake in homebuilder Lennar

The move

Berkshire Hathaway bought 2.67 million Lennar Class A shares and 75,021 Class B shares between Sept. 17 and Sept. 21, worth about $212.4 million, taking its total position to nearly 10% of Lennar and about $1.8 billion. The buying started a day after Lennar missed third-quarter earnings estimates.

Why it matters

It's Berkshire's second housing bet under CEO Greg Abel, after it agreed to buy homebuilder Taylor Morrison for about $6.8 billion in a deal that closed in July.

The bear case

Lennar's stock fell on the earnings miss that preceded Berkshire's buying, and homebuilders are still working through weak demand and high mortgage rates.

What to watch

Whether Berkshire keeps adding to the Lennar stake, and Lennar's next earnings report.
