Marvell falls 8% in broad AI chip selloff

The move

Marvell shares fell about 8% on Oct. 22 to $224.64 in morning trading, part of a semiconductor-wide selloff triggered by chipmaking-equipment supplier Lam Research, which warned on Oct. 21 that supply constraints were limiting its own revenue guidance.

Why it matters

The selloff hit a stock Charles Koch's family holds as an investment, underscoring how sensitive AI-chip investors remain to supply-chain warnings.

The bear case

Marvell had already rallied sharply this year; some of the drop looks like profit-taking after a steep run-up rather than a change in its own business.

What to watch

Whether other chip-equipment makers confirm or ease Lam Research's supply warning in their own results.

Marvell slides as a chip-equipment maker flags supply strain

The move

Marvell Technology shares fell about 8% on Thursday after chipmaking-equipment supplier Lam Research said on its Oct. 21 earnings call that near-term supply-chain constraints would otherwise have lifted its revenue guidance. AMD and Intel also fell that day, down about 8% and 7%, and the iShares Semiconductor ETF dropped 6%.

Why it matters

Lam's comments raised worries that supply limits, not demand, could cap how fast AI-chip makers like Marvell can grow, after a stretch of steep valuations across the sector.

The bear case

Lam's own executives said the constraints should ease, with CEO Tim Archer predicting the industry will be "caught up very shortly."

What to watch

Marvell's next earnings report for signs the supply strain is easing.
