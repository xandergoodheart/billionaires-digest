Reliance's Jio Platforms opens India's largest-ever IPO

The move

Jio Platforms opened its initial public offering to the public on Oct. 21, with a price band of 1,065 to 1,119 rupees per share. At the top of that band the fresh-issue offering would raise about 30,213 crore rupees (roughly $3.6 billion), which Indian financial press describe as the country's largest-ever IPO by issue size. Subscription closes Oct. 23. Reliance Industries holds more than 66% of Jio Platforms, with a Meta affiliate near 10% and Google holding about 7.7%, according to LSEG data.

Why it matters

A successful listing would give Ambani's Reliance a large new source of capital and a public market valuation for its telecom and digital arm.

The bear case

The final issue price and allotment results won't be known until subscription closes, and Reliance retains firm control regardless of how the public portion prices.

What to watch

Jio Platforms' subscription numbers when the window closes Oct. 23, and its stock-market debut.

Reliance pays staff early ahead of record Jio IPO

The move

Ahead of Jio Platforms' IPO, Reliance paid October salaries to its nearly 420,000 group employees about 15 days early and encouraged them to invest, without saying why, according to a Bloomberg report. The IPO's anchor book opens Oct. 19 and public bidding runs Oct. 21-23, targeting up to 30,200 crore rupees (about $3.2 billion) at a price band of 1,065 to 1,119 rupees a share, valuing Jio at up to 10.3 lakh crore rupees (about $108 billion). Up to 500 crore rupees of shares are set aside for eligible Jio employees at a 5% discount.

Why it matters

It's one of India's largest-ever IPOs, and the early pay and outreach show how hard Reliance is pushing to build demand beyond big institutional investors.

The bear case

Encouraging employees to put personal savings into a company-linked stock concentrates their financial risk in one bet.

What to watch

Jio Platforms' public subscription window, Oct. 21-23.

Jio Platforms nears India's biggest-ever IPO

The move

Reliance's Jio Platforms filed its draft IPO prospectus with India's securities regulator SEBI on June 19, 2026 and received SEBI's approval on Aug. 28. The offering is reported at about 37,700 crore rupees (roughly $4 billion), entirely new shares, with proceeds going mainly to repay Jio debt, Angel One reported. Roadshows wrapped up in September and October, led by Akash Ambani, who called the listing "a great responsibility" and the Reliance group's first public share sale in nearly three decades, alongside his sister Isha Ambani.

Why it matters

A Jio listing of this size would be India's largest IPO on record and a major test of investor appetite for its telecom-and-digital business separate from parent Reliance Industries.

The bear case

The exact subscription dates, price band and listing date are still not formally confirmed by Jio or the exchanges, so the terms could still shift before launch.

What to watch

Jio's expected subscription window around Oct. 21-23 and a listing near Oct. 28.

Ambani's Jio Platforms sets Oct. 21 date for $3.8B IPO

The move

Jio Platforms, the telecom and digital arm of Mukesh Ambani's Reliance Industries, plans to launch its IPO on Oct. 21, targeting about $3.8 billion in what would be India's largest listing to date, surpassing Hyundai Motor India's $2.9 billion IPO in 2024. Shares are expected to begin trading Oct. 28. The offering covers up to 270 million shares, about 2.9% of Jio's post-issue share capital, with most proceeds earmarked to repay debt owed by Reliance Jio Infocomm.

Why it matters

A listing this size is a major test of investor appetite for India's telecom and digital sector, and it starts to put a public market price on one of Ambani's most valuable units.

The bear case

The IPO is mainly aimed at paying down Jio's own telecom debt rather than raising new growth capital, so a strong listing doesn't necessarily signal fresh expansion.

What to watch

Investor demand and pricing once the roadshow begins ahead of the Oct. 21 launch.

The move

Reliance's Jio Platforms is preparing an IPO targeting about $3.8 billion, with a launch planned around October 21 and listing around October 28.
