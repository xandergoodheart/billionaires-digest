The move

Why it matters

A credit-rating warning is a different kind of alarm than a stock drop: it signals that bond investors, not just equity traders, are starting to question the AI buildout's finances, right as Nvidia's board is still investigating a foreign-intelligence matter involving Jensen Huang.

The bear case

What to watch
