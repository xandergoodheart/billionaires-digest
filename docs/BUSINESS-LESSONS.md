# How news-plus-product businesses grow, and what Billionaires Digest can borrow

## A. Summary

Every company studied runs the same loop. A free daily habit (a newsletter, a puzzle, scores, a streak) brings people back each day. The same data powers both the story and the product. Once people come back often, they get a reason to sign up (an account, a league, a watchlist). Money comes from things that sit next to that habit: a subscription bundle, a partner fee, ads or sponsors. Paid games of chance are almost always run by a licensed partner, not by the media brand. ESPN is the clearest case. It kept its own brand but handed the licensed betting business first to PENN and, from December 2025, to DraftKings. Fantasy stays free and sits inside the ESPN app. The NYT uses free daily games as its entry point and sells a bundle. Morning Brew and Robinhood Snacks show that one free daily email can grow to millions of readers through referrals. Kalshi and Polymarket show that live odds shown inside other outlets' news is a distribution channel in its own right. For Billionaires Digest, that points to a free morning email as the main growth engine, a daily mini-game as the habit, "follow a billionaire" alerts as the reason to create an account, and later a Plus tier with no gambling in it. Keep any future real-money play fully separate and run by a licensed partner, as ESPN does. The risks are real. Robinhood paid $7.5M over "gamification" (confetti, scratch-off rewards, push nudges). States are fighting prediction markets over under-21 users and licensing.

## B. Company by company

**ESPN (news + fantasy + betting partner + subscription)**
- **Betting through a partner, not in-house.** In 2023 PENN paid about $2B over 10 years to use the "ESPN BET" name. The two sides ended the deal in November 2025, saying it fell short of performance targets. DraftKings became ESPN's exclusive official sportsbook and odds provider from Dec 1, 2025, with full rollout in 2026. The ESPN BET name now covers betting content only. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/06/disneys-espn-penn-entertainment-to-wind-down-espn-bet.html), [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/sports-betting/2025/penn-espn-bet-termination-draftkings-1234876039/), [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/espn-bet-draftkings-deal-1236571246/))
- **Free fantasy keeps people coming back.** A record 14M+ people played ESPN Fantasy Football in 2025, the third straight year of growth above 1M. Week 1 Sunday was the busiest day ever in the fantasy app. ([ESPN Press Room](https://espnpressroom.com/us/press-releases/2025/09/all-time-record-four-years-in-a-row-14-million-fans-playing-espn-fantasy-football-in-2025/))
- **One app for everything.** The subscription service launched Aug 21, 2025 at $29.99/month (or $11.99 for the smaller "Select" plan). Fantasy, betting info, stats and commerce all live inside the same app. It also has a personalized, AI-assisted SportsCenter. ([Disney IR](https://investors.thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/news-details/2025/ESPNs-Direct-to-Consumer-Service-and-Enhanced-App-Launching-August-21/default.aspx))
- **Lesson.** A strong media brand can put its name on a licensed operator. But the ESPN–PENN deal shows that a brand alone doesn't guarantee betting results.

**Yahoo Finance (news + portfolio tools + paid tiers)**
- **A huge free audience.** Yahoo reports about 93M monthly US visitors (figure repeated by third-party stat sites; Yahoo's own ad materials are the original source, so treat it as approximate). ([Search Endurance](https://searchendurance.com/yahoo-statistics/))
- **Free tools create accounts.** Watchlists, multiple portfolios and linked brokerage accounts are free on every plan. Those features give people a reason to log in and return.
- **Paid tiers.** Bronze ($9.95/month) removes ads and adds portfolio analysis. Silver ($24.95/month) adds research, fair value, stock picks and FT/MT Newswire news. Gold adds more, including 40 years of downloadable data. Paid value = no ads + deeper data + alerts. ([Yahoo Help](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/yahoo-finance-plus/select-plans-sln36623.html), [Smart Investor](https://thesmartinvestor.com/investing-tools/yahoo-finance-subscription/))

**Robinhood (learning + newsletter + subscription + prediction markets)**
- **Content brings people in.** Robinhood's first-ever acquisition (March 2019) was MarketSnacks, a free daily newsletter and podcast. It was renamed Robinhood Snacks, and the founders stayed on as editors. A third-party case study says it reached about 36M email subscribers by June 2021 (not from Robinhood itself; treat as approximate). ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2019/03/25/robinhood-acquires-marketsnacks), [RightMetric](https://rightmetric.co/outsight-library/pro-members-how-robinhood-created-a-newsletter-with-36-million-subscribers))
- **Learning hub.** Robinhood Learn has hundreds of free articles, from "Investing 101" to options, plus in-app lessons. ([Robinhood Learn](https://learn.robinhood.com/), [Newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/learn-and-grow-with-robinhood))
- **Subscription.** Robinhood Gold reached a record 3.5M subscribers in Q2 2025. ([Robinhood Q2 2025](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-second-quarter-2025-results))
- **Prediction markets.** The hub launched March 17, 2025, routed through Kalshi, a CFTC-regulated exchange. Reports put it at more than 12B contracts in the first nine months and about 8.8B in Q1 2026 (figures from secondary sources). ([Robinhood Newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-prediction-markets-hub/), [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/how-prediction-markets-scaled-to-usd-21b-in-monthly-volume-in-2026))
- **The warning.** In January 2024 Robinhood paid Massachusetts $7.5M to settle a "gamification" case. The state cited confetti, lists of popular stocks, push notifications and scratch-off-style free-stock rewards. Robinhood agreed to stop using confetti permanently and to overhaul how it nudges users. ([Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2024/01/19/756246.htm), [V&E](https://www.velaw.com/insights/game-over-robinhood-pays-7-5-million-to-resolve-gamification-securities-violations/))

**The New York Times (the bundle)**
- **Games are the way in.** NYT Games puzzles were played 11.2B times in 2025. Wordle brought in a younger, more international audience, and the Times then steers those players to other games and to news. ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/the-next-level-for-us-the-new-york-times-eyes-longer-play-sessions-for-games-in-subscription-drive/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/new-york-times-earnings-wordle-acquisition-users-0cb11458-5b27-493b-a14b-d0d96eb82f7a.html))
- **A calm daily habit.** Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. The stated aim is a healthy daily habit, not endless screen time.
- **The bundle pays.** 13.4M digital subscribers as of Q2 2026, with digital subscription revenue up 16.4% to $408M. Earlier, 52% of digital subscribers were on a multi-product bundle. A family plan costs $30/month, or $10/month for games only. Bonus puzzles are for subscribers only. ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/884355/new-york-times-adds-280000-digital-subscribers-as-q2-revenue-climbs-1/), [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/09/the-new-york-times-launches-a-family-subscription-with-separate-wordles-for-everyone/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/erikkain/2026/09/02/nyt-games-bonus-puzzles-wordle-in-1-connections-3x3/))
- *Uncertain:* a claim that "110,000 new Q1 2025 subscribers came from Games" appears only in a secondary blog. I could not confirm it.

**Morning Brew (newsletter first)**
- **Referrals drive growth.** Referrals bring in about 30% of new subscribers each month. Every issue has a "Share the Brew" block with rewards at set referral counts: mostly branded merchandise, plus a premium newsletter at 3 referrals. ([SparkLoop](https://sparkloop.app/blog/the-secrets-behind-morning-brews-growth-to-2-million-newsletter-subscribers-6), [ReferralCandy](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/morning-brew-referral-program))
- **Revenue.** Mostly sponsorships and ads. It had 3M subscribers when it sold a majority stake at a $75M valuation (2020) and 4M+ by 2022. Axel Springer took full ownership in February 2025. Revenue of about $70M in 2025 is reported but unconfirmed. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/03/28/morning-brew-tops-4-million-subscribers-as-it-looks-to-expand-with-ma.html), [Talking Biz News](https://talkingbiznews.com/media-news/axel-springer-buys-remaining-stake-in-morning-brew/))

**Sleeper (social fantasy)**
- **Chat is the product.** Chat, voice messages, GIFs and trash talk are built into every league. Users reportedly open the app 4–5 times a day, and up to 30 minutes on game days (from secondary sources). ([DraftKick](https://draftkick.com/blog/story-of-sleeper/), [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Sleeper_fantasy_sports_app))
- **New formats keep it fresh.** Chopped (weekly elimination) leagues reportedly have 2M+ players.
- **Money comes from paid contests, and so do the legal fights.** Paid Picks contests are blocked in about 20 states. California's attorney general said in July 2025 that pick'em-style fantasy is illegal there. Sleeper also sued the CFTC over its prediction-markets application. ([RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/fantasy/sleeper-promo-code/legal-states), [WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20260205-legal-developments-in-the-gaming-industry-second-half-of-2025))

**Duolingo (streaks, leagues, free with paid upgrade)**
- **Scale.** 50M+ daily users in Q3 2025 and 56.5M in Q1 2026. 10.3M paid subscribers in Q1 2025. Over $1B in bookings in 2025. ([Duolingo IR](https://investors.duolingo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-surpasses-50-million-daily-active-users-grows-dau-36), [Q4 2025 letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/961ce633-3cee-49d0-bd7a-2c63731d45fb))
- **Streaks with forgiveness.** More than half of daily learners have a streak of at least 7 days. Streak "freezes" and repairs mean one missed day doesn't wipe out months of progress. Duolingo found two freezes worked better than one, three were no better, and too much forgiveness weakens the habit. ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals/))
- **Weekly leagues.** Leagues reportedly raised lesson completion by 25% (secondary source).

**Kalshi / Polymarket (odds as a media product)**
- **Kalshi and CNN.** From December 2025 Kalshi is CNN's official prediction-market partner. CNN pulls live odds through Kalshi's feed for an on-air ticker and graphics and does not pay for the data. CNBC also uses Kalshi data. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/12/02/cnn-kalshi-prediction-market-data), [Kalshi](https://news.kalshi.com/p/kalshi-cnn-prediction-market-partnership))
- **Polymarket.** Official partner of X (June 2025). Also has a Dow Jones/WSJ data deal, Substack embeds for writers, and a Perplexity integration. The payoff is free distribution for the operator. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/06/x-names-polymarket-as-its-official-prediction-market-partner/), [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/02/polymarket-says-journalism-is-better-when-its-backed-by-live-markets-does-anyone-know-what-that-means/))
- **Legal pushback.** Several states (NV, NJ, MD, TN, CT, among others) sent cease-and-desist orders or sued over sports contracts, partly over bans on bettors under 21. Court rulings conflict. Kalshi added age checks. ([RotoWire timeline](https://www.rotowire.com/prediction-markets/legal-timeline), [CT AG](https://portal.ct.gov/ag/press-releases/2026-press-releases/connecticut-sues-kalshi-to-stop-illegal-unlicensed-sports-betting))

## C. The common formula

1. **One free daily ritual:** the NYT puzzle, Brew's email, the Duolingo streak, fantasy lineup lock. It is small, the same for everyone, and time-boxed.
2. **One data engine, many surfaces:** ESPN's stats feed the story, fantasy scoring and betting odds. Kalshi's prices feed CNN's graphics.
3. **An account earns its keep:** a watchlist, a league, a streak or a portfolio gives people a reason to sign up.
4. **Social pressure and status:** leagues, group chat, leaderboards, shareable results (Wordle's grid).
5. **Referrals and embeds spread it for free:** Brew's referral rewards, Polymarket's embeds, Substack writers.
6. **Money sits next to the habit:** bundle or Plus tier (NYT, Yahoo, Gold, Duolingo), sponsorship (Brew), partner fees (ESPN–DraftKings). Anything involving real-money chance is run by a licensed third party.
7. **Forgiveness beats pressure:** streak freezes and "one a day" design keep people coming back without the nudges regulators punish.

**Top 5 priorities, in order**
1. The morning email (#1). It reuses daily work and is the main growth channel.
2. "Guess the Move" daily game with streaks (#3). It is the daily habit.
3. Follow a billionaire + alerts (#4). It is the reason to create an account.
4. Referral rewards (#2), added once the email is running.
5. Weekly recap cards (#6) and odds inside stories (#5). Both are cheap links between the sections.

The Plus tier (#10) comes after there is an engaged audience, as the NYT and Duolingo did.

**What to avoid**
- **Robinhood-style nudges.** No confetti celebrations, scratch-off reveals or "hot stocks" lists tied to real trading. Push notifications must not urge trading. The Massachusetts consent order ([V&E](https://www.velaw.com/insights/game-over-robinhood-pays-7-5-million-to-resolve-gamification-securities-violations/)) is the checklist.
  - The **Lucky Five slot spinner** is the riskiest-looking feature. Keep it clearly cosmetic.
  - Consider dropping slot-machine sound and visual cues near anything tied to real stocks.
- **Minors next to betting.** The Academy will attract students. States fight Kalshi over under-21 users, and The Book and Next Moves look like betting.
  - Put an 18+ gate (21+ is safer) on The Book and Next Moves.
  - Keep the Learn section usable without them.
  - If any user could be under 13, COPPA applies. Don't knowingly collect data from under-13s.
- **Anything that makes play-coins worth money.** Buying coins, cashing out, prizes, or Plus boosting game odds all create "consideration + chance + prize", which is gambling or sweepstakes territory. California is already hostile to pick'em fantasy and sweepstakes ([WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20260205-legal-developments-in-the-gaming-industry-second-half-of-2025)).
- **Folding real money into the main brand too early.** ESPN–PENN shows that even a huge brand deal can miss its targets. When real money comes, follow the ESPN–DraftKings model: a licensed partner, a separate product, and content kept apart from the operator. Get legal review first.
- **Blurring facts and opinion.** Odds, AI reads and "school of thought" lessons must be clearly labeled as opinion or play forecasts, never mixed into "The move".
