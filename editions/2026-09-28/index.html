Lede of the day

Wall Street starts pricing in doubts about the AI spending boom

Mark Zuckerberg lost $9 billion in a day after Goldman Sachs questioned whether AI hyperscalers can ever earn back their spending, the same week Larry Ellison and Masayoshi Son each leaned harder on debt to keep funding their own AI-era bets.

Mark ZuckerbergIPO / MarketsAI & techMETAAI read · ▼ Reality check

Zuckerberg loses $9 billion in a day on AI doubts

The move

Meta shares fell about 4% on Sept. 25, cutting Zuckerberg's Forbes-estimated net worth by $8.9 billion to $257.5 billion, after Goldman Sachs said AI hyperscalers need roughly $300 billion a year in AI-services revenue just to break even on their infrastructure spending, and about $1 trillion in AI-application spending for solid returns.

Why it matters

It was the single biggest one-day billionaire loss on the Forbes list that day and dropped Zuckerberg from 4th to 6th on the real-time rankings, a sign Wall Street is starting to question whether AI capex will pay for itself.

The bear case

Meta shares had surged roughly 36% in September on the strength of its new Muse AI assistant; a one-day pullback after a big run isn't proof investors are turning on the AI trade.

What to watch

Meta's next AI-spending commentary, and whether Goldman's hyperscaler break-even math becomes the market's general yardstick for AI stocks.

Larry EllisonFinancingAI & techORCLAI read · — Leverage play

Ellison pledges another $9.2 billion in Oracle stock

The move

An Oracle proxy filing disclosed Sept. 25 that Larry Ellison has pledged 67 million more Oracle shares as collateral for personal loans, a 19% increase from 2025, worth about $9.2 billion at Friday's $137.10 closing price.

Why it matters

The pledges help secure loans backing the roughly $111 billion Paramount Skydance deal to buy Warner Bros. Discovery, which Ellison and his son David are financing with about $47 billion of equity, partly from Middle Eastern sovereign funds.

The bear case

Pledged stock is collateral, not a sale, but a sharp enough drop in Oracle's share price could force Ellison to post more shares or cash to his lenders.

What to watch

Whether Ellison pledges further shares as the Paramount Skydance-Warner Bros. Discovery deal moves toward closing.

Masayoshi SonIPO / MarketsAI & tech9984.TAI read · — Expensive conviction

SoftBank sells record $11.1 billion of bonds for its OpenAI bet

The move

SoftBank is selling $11 billion of bonds this week — $10 billion in dollar notes across three maturities plus €1 billion in euro notes across two — to partially fund a follow-on OpenAI investment closing next month, on top of nearly $65 billion it has already committed to the startup. It's SoftBank's biggest junk-bond deal of the year after a string of other debt raises, including a $5 billion expansion of its Arm Holdings margin loan.

Why it matters

It shows how much Masayoshi Son is willing to borrow, and at what cost, to keep funding OpenAI: yields on the notes are among the richest SoftBank has ever paid, and its 2031 dollar bond now yields 8.2%, up from 6.7% in January.

The bear case

Piling debt on debt to fund a stake in a company that still isn't public is a bet that OpenAI's eventual valuation outruns SoftBank's rising borrowing costs.

What to watch

Whether the bond deal closes at its full size and whether OpenAI's IPO timeline slips further.

Bernard Arnault & familyRestructuringLuxury & retailMC.PA · CDI.PAAI read · ▲ Succession planning

Arnault family simplifies LVMH control before generational handoff

The move

Christian Dior said Sept. 23 the Arnault family will merge its holding companies into Christian Dior, convert it into a partnership renamed Agache SCA, and hold 49.76% of LVMH's capital and 65.55% of its voting rights. Dior shares jumped about 17% on the news. Shareholders vote in December 2026, with a mandatory cash tender offer for remaining Dior shares to follow in the first quarter of 2027 at 95% of Dior's net asset value.

Why it matters

Folding decades of cross-holdings into one listed vehicle makes it simpler for Arnault to pass control of the world's biggest luxury group to his children without a fight over who holds which entity.

The bear case

Minority Dior shareholders could still push back on the tender price, and the plan still needs sign-off from France's securities regulator, the AMF.

What to watch

The extraordinary shareholder meetings in December and the AMF's review of the first-quarter 2027 tender offer.

Warren BuffettInvestmentReal estateBRK.A · LENAI read · ▲ Buying the dip

Berkshire nearly doubles its stake in homebuilder Lennar

The move

Berkshire Hathaway disclosed Sept. 26 that it now owns 25.9 million Lennar shares worth about $2.1 billion, up from 13.4 million shares as of June 30, after buying more than $348 million worth between Sept. 17 and Sept. 21.

Why it matters

Lennar's stock has slumped this year on a weak housing market, and Berkshire adding aggressively into that weakness is a classic bet that the market has overcorrected on homebuilders.

The bear case

At roughly $2.1 billion, the position is still small next to Berkshire's cash pile, and it's reportedly the work of portfolio manager Ted Weschler rather than Buffett himself.

What to watch

Whether Berkshire keeps adding to Lennar, and whether the homebuilder's results confirm a housing-market bottom.

Jensen HuangPolicyAI & techNVDA · BABAAI read · ▲ Cautious optimism

China signals it may let Alibaba, ByteDance buy new Nvidia chip

The move

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has asked Alibaba and ByteDance about their planned orders for Nvidia's new RTX Pro 5500 workstation chip, and officials have signaled the purchases are likely to be approved, The Information reported Sept. 27. Nothing is finalized.

Why it matters

The RTX Pro 5500, an 84GB Blackwell-based card, was built to sit under U.S. export-control thresholds; a Chinese green light would reopen a major market Nvidia has largely lost to export curbs.

The bear case

This is a signal relayed by unnamed officials, not confirmed policy, and Beijing has reversed similar chip-buying openings before.

What to watch

Formal confirmation from MIIT and whether Alibaba or ByteDance actually place orders.

Gautam AdaniInvestmentIndustrialsADANIENT.NSAI read · ▲ Long-range bet

Adani pledges ₹1 lakh crore for West Bengal by 2035

The move

Gautam Adani announced Sept. 24 his group will invest more than ₹1 lakh crore (100,000 crore rupees) in West Bengal by 2035 across ports, power, roads, green cement and data centers, plus over ₹4,000 crore for a 2,000-bed hospital, the Adani Arogya Mandir.

Why it matters

It's one of Adani's largest state-level commitments and extends his ports-and-infrastructure empire into a state where his group has historically had less presence.

The bear case

Adani has made large multi-year state investment pledges before; with a timeline stretching to 2035, how much is new capital versus long-range aspiration will only show up over years.

What to watch

Ground-breaking and first-phase spending on the Bengal projects and the linked hospital.

Aliko DangoteInvestmentEnergyPrivateAI read · ▲ Ambitious build-out

Dangote says refinery expansion to 1.4 million barrels on track

The move

Aliko Dangote said Sept. 24 his group is on track to double capacity at its Lekki mega-refinery to 1.4 million barrels a day by 2029, in a $14.3 billion expansion designed to be running before the original plant needs major maintenance in 2030.

Why it matters

It would make Dangote's complex one of the world's largest refining sites as a global fuel-supply crunch boosts refining profits, the same month he opened an IPO for the refinery business.

The bear case

Megaprojects on this scale routinely slip; Dangote's original refinery itself took years longer and cost far more than first announced.

What to watch

Progress on the second Lekki unit and the Sept. 30 groundbreaking for Dangote's separate Lamu, Kenya refinery.

Mukesh AmbaniIPO / MarketsFinanceRELIANCE.NSAI read · — Racing the clock

Reliance lines up second $1 billion bond sale in three weeks

The move

Reliance Industries is planning to raise about 100 billion rupees ($1 billion) in 10-year bonds at a 7.90% coupon, its second local-currency bond sale in three weeks after a 120 billion rupee, 7.47%-coupon deal two weeks earlier, adding about $2.3 billion in fresh rupee debt.

Why it matters

Bankers say Reliance is racing to price the debt before the Reserve Bank of India's Oct. 7 policy decision, in case the central bank drains liquidity and pushes borrowing costs higher.

The bear case

Back-to-back borrowing pushes Reliance's outstanding bonds to 540 billion rupees; if rates fall anyway after the RBI decision, Reliance will have locked in higher costs for nothing.

What to watch

The bond pricing this week and the RBI's Oct. 7 policy decision.

Elon MuskProduct launchAerospacePrivateAI read · ▲ High-stakes test

SpaceX clears FAA hurdle for Starship's biggest test yet

The move

The FAA gave SpaceX final approval on Sept. 26 for Starship Flight 14, an orbital test that will deploy 26 Starlink V3 satellites for the first time, using Ship 41 and Super Heavy Booster 21. The flight is set for Monday, Sept. 28, after a wet dress rehearsal and a delay tied to public-safety concerns.

Why it matters

A clean flight would prove Starship V3 can carry a full Starlink payload and reenter intact, a key step in SpaceX's push to keep launching at a pace that supports its roughly $1.95 trillion valuation.

The bear case

SpaceX already scrubbed and rescheduled this exact flight once this month; another delay or a failed reentry would raise fresh doubts about V3's reliability.

What to watch

Whether Flight 14 launches Monday and whether both stages survive reentry.
