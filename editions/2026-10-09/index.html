Lede of the day

Musk and Ambani's Starlink feud goes public

Elon Musk publicly blamed Indian "oligarchs" for blocking Starlink's launch in India, reigniting his long-running rivalry with Mukesh Ambani over satellite spectrum.

Elon MuskPolicyAerospacePrivateAI read · — Escalating

Musk blames "oligarchs" as Starlink stays stuck in India

The move

Musk said Starlink still cannot launch in India despite years of trying to meet the government's legal and licensing requirements, and he blamed unnamed "oligarchs" with a "monopolistic chokehold" for the holdup.

Why it matters

Musk's remark is widely read as aimed at Mukesh Ambani's Reliance Jio, which has sparred with Starlink over how India allocates satellite spectrum since 2023, even after Jio and Starlink struck a distribution partnership in 2025.

The bear case

Starlink and Jio are still nominally partners on distribution, so an all-out price war is not guaranteed even if the public rhetoric escalates.

What to watch

Whether India's government moves to unfreeze Starlink's commercial launch.

Musk back above $1 trillion as SpaceX stock jumps

The move

SpaceX shares climbed to their highest level since June, and stronger Tesla delivery numbers added to the move, pushing Musk's combined net worth back above $1 trillion.

Why it matters

Musk's fortune is now overwhelmingly tied to two stocks, so a one-day rally in SpaceX can swing his net worth by tens of billions of dollars.

The bear case

SpaceX shares are still well below their mid-June intraday record, and Musk briefly lost trillionaire status once already this year when the stock pulled back.

What to watch

SpaceX's next quarterly earnings and Starship test flight, both expected later in October.

Warren BuffettInvestmentFinanceBRK.A · LENAI read · ▲ Contrarian

Berkshire keeps buying Lennar as homebuilder slides

The move

Berkshire Hathaway disclosed it bought roughly 2.4 million more Lennar shares in a pair of October trades, pushing its stake in the homebuilder to about 11-12% even as a short-seller report pressured Lennar's stock.

Why it matters

Crossing the 10% ownership threshold means Berkshire must now disclose its Lennar trades promptly instead of waiting for a quarterly filing, giving a rare real-time window into one of its biggest recent bets.

The bear case

Lennar's own guidance points to a softer home-sales environment, and buying into a stock under short-seller pressure can take years to pay off.

What to watch

Whether Berkshire keeps adding to the stake or pauses after crossing the reporting threshold.

Liang WenfengInvestmentAI & techPrivateAI read · ▲ Expansion

DeepSeek's new funding round swells toward $15 billion

The move

DeepSeek is close to securing at least 80 billion yuan (about $12 billion) in a new funding round that could reach roughly 100 billion yuan (about $15 billion) based on signed term sheets, with Tencent and battery maker CATL committing the largest amounts.

Why it matters

The round would value founder Liang Wenfeng's AI lab far above its first outside round earlier in 2026, and ties two of China's biggest corporate names, Tencent's Ma Huateng and CATL's Robin Zeng, directly to its growth.

The bear case

Neither DeepSeek, Tencent nor CATL has publicly confirmed the terms, and the round was reportedly paused once already after comments from Liang about reliance on Nvidia chips went viral.

What to watch

Whether the round closes near $15 billion and whether DeepSeek proceeds with a flagged 2027 Shanghai STAR Market listing.

Nvidia shares hit records, closing in on $6 trillion

The move

Nvidia shares hit a fresh record high on October 2 and kept climbing into the following week, pushing its market capitalization to roughly $5.7-5.84 trillion, helped by a $150 billion increase to its stock buyback program that the company called the largest single additional buyback authorization in corporate history.

Why it matters

Nvidia is already the world's most valuable company, and the buyback signals the board sees the AI-chip rally as durable rather than overextended.

The bear case

Reaching the $6 trillion milestone requires the stock to keep climbing from already-record levels, and any stumble in AI-spending sentiment, like the OpenAI-linked wobble this past week, could stall the run.

What to watch

Whether Nvidia's share price pushes the company's market cap past the $6 trillion mark.

Tesla deliveries beat estimates despite tax-credit hangover

The move

Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles in the third quarter, beating its own analyst-compiled consensus of about 461,974, even though the total came in about 2% below the year-ago quarter that had been boosted by buyers rushing ahead of the expiring federal EV tax credit.

Why it matters

The beat eases pressure on Tesla heading into its October 21 earnings call, where Musk is expected to lay out the company's AI and robotics plans.

The bear case

Energy storage deployments missed expectations, and deliveries still fell year-over-year, so the "beat" is relative to lowered expectations rather than outright growth.

What to watch

Tesla's full third-quarter financial results, due after market close on October 21.

Jeff BezosInvestmentAI & techAMZNAI read · — Defensive

Amazon pledges $1B to data-center host communities

The move

Amazon launched a program called Built Together, committing more than $1 billion over five years to communities hosting its U.S. data centers, covering workforce training, energy-efficiency upgrades and flexible local funding, on top of over $1 billion it says it already spent on such communities in the past three years.

Why it matters

AWS CEO Matt Garman said more than 100 local moratoriums on new data centers are under consideration nationwide, so the spending is aimed at easing community pushback that could otherwise slow Amazon's AI buildout.

The bear case

A $1 billion pledge is small next to Amazon's overall data-center capital spending, and it does not address the underlying power and water strain that is driving local opposition.

What to watch

Whether other hyperscalers announce similar community-investment programs as data-center opposition spreads.

Elon MuskIPO / MarketsAI & techPrivateAI read · ▲ Concentration risk

Tech billionaires added $845B in 2026, Musk a third of it

The move

The Bloomberg Billionaires Index found that roughly 100 tech billionaires added a combined $845 billion in wealth through September, while billionaires outside tech lost $62 billion as a group; Musk alone accounted for roughly $310 billion of the tech gain, tied mainly to SpaceX's rise.

Why it matters

The split shows just how concentrated 2026's wealth creation has been in AI and space-linked tech stocks rather than spread across industries.

The bear case

Index-based wealth figures swing with daily stock prices, so a single bad week for SpaceX or Tesla could erase a large share of Musk's reported gain.

What to watch

Whether the gap between tech and non-tech billionaire wealth keeps widening into year-end.
