SoftBank prices record $11.1B bond for OpenAI stake

The move

SoftBank priced $11.1 billion in dollar- and euro-denominated bonds on September 24, the largest high-yield corporate bond sale on record, with yields as high as 9.75%. The money funds a roughly $10 billion installment toward SoftBank's approximately $64.6 billion investment in OpenAI, which is due to close October 1.

Why it matters

It shows how much debt Son is willing to take on to fund SoftBank's AI bets, and investors are still lining up: SoftBank shares jumped more than 7% on the news when Japanese markets reopened.

The bear case

Paying up to 9.75% shows lenders are charging a steep premium given SoftBank's growing debt load and OpenAI's still-distant IPO.

What to watch

The bond's settlement on September 29 and the OpenAI investment closing October 1.

Mark ZuckerbergProduct launchAI & techMETAAI read · ▲ Bullish

Meta stock jumps 11% in a day on Muse AI buzz

The move

Meta shares rose 11% in a single day and closed near a 52-week high after its Muse AI assistant, launched September 8, topped app-store charts with more than 2.5 million downloads. The move added over $100 billion to Meta's market cap, pushing its value close to $1.9 trillion.

Why it matters

Muse becoming a hit app is the market's first real evidence that Meta's massive AI spending can show up in a product people actually use, not just in ad-targeting algorithms.

The bear case

Most Muse features are still free, and it's unproven whether app-store buzz and downloads convert into paying subscribers at Meta's $20 or $100 monthly tiers.

What to watch

Whether Muse subscription uptake shows up in Meta's next earnings report.

Larry EllisonInsider tradeAI & techORCLAI read · — Leverage

Ellison pledges $9.2B more Oracle stock as collateral

The move

A proxy filing disclosed Friday that Larry Ellison has pledged 67 million more Oracle shares as loan collateral than a year earlier, a 19% increase. Those shares are worth about $9.2 billion at Oracle's Friday close of $137.10, bringing his total pledged stake to roughly 36% of his approximately 1.16 billion Oracle shares.

Why it matters

The added borrowing coincides with his family's role financing Paramount Skydance's acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, showing how much of Ellison's fortune is tied up as loan collateral rather than cash.

The bear case

Pledging shares as collateral is common for founders and isn't a sale; Oracle stock would need to fall sharply before lenders could force a margin call.

What to watch

Whether Ellison discloses further share pledges as the Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery financing progresses.

Elon MuskPolicyMediaPrivateAI read · — Escalation

US backs Musk's court fight against EU's $137M fine on X

The move

The US Justice Department filed Thursday to support Elon Musk and X's bid to have the European Commission's €120 million ($137 million) Digital Services Act fine thrown out at the EU's General Court. The Commission issued the fine in December 2025 over X's paid blue-checkmark verification, its ad database and limits on researcher access to public data.

Why it matters

It turns one company's regulatory fight into a US-versus-EU dispute over who gets to police American tech platforms operating in Europe.

The bear case

The EU said Friday it's ready to defend the fine vigorously in court, and Washington's support doesn't change the transparency violations the Commission cited.

What to watch

Whether the EU General Court sets a hearing date for the joint challenge from X and Musk.

Changpeng ZhaoIPO / MarketsFinancePrivateAI read · ▲ Volatile

Binance's Zhao gains $9.8B in a day as crypto rebounds

The move

Changpeng Zhao's estimated net worth rose $9.76 billion in 24 hours to $91.2 billion on September 20, a roughly 12% one-day gain tied to the rising estimated value of his Binance stake. His fortune is up $41.5 billion, or 83.6%, since the start of the year, per the Bloomberg Billionaires Index.

Why it matters

Zhao says capital that rotated into AI stocks this year is starting to flow back into crypto, and swings this large show how tightly his fortune tracks Binance's implied valuation.

The bear case

Most of Zhao's wealth is an estimate of a private company's value, not cash or public shares, so a one-day, double-digit swing says more about valuation models than liquid wealth.

What to watch

Whether the crypto rally Zhao is describing continues or fades.

Jensen HuangProduct launchAI & techNVDAAI read · ▲ Bullish

Huang says Nvidia could double chip sales next year

The move

Speaking in Scotland on September 19, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang said the company could sell twice as many chips next year as this year, driven by demand for its new Vera Rubin architecture. Wall Street estimates put that near $700 billion in fiscal 2028 revenue, up about 70% from an expected $411 billion in fiscal 2027.

Why it matters

Huang says every frontier AI company plans to adopt Vera Rubin at launch, unlike the prior Blackwell Ultra generation, which is why he's more confident about next year's growth.

The bear case

Doubling chip sales assumes AI infrastructure spending keeps accelerating at its current pace, with no pullback from hyperscaler customers.

What to watch

Nvidia's next earnings call for updated Vera Rubin order and shipment numbers.

Liang WenfengProduct launchAI & techPrivateAI read · — Technical

DeepSeek details an AI training system built for scale

The move

DeepSeek published a paper on September 23 describing DSec, an internal system for managing sandboxes used to train AI agents. A single production unit spans about 160 nodes, supports roughly 3 million sandboxes a day and can create more than 5,000 sandboxes per second. Founder Liang Wenfeng is listed among the paper's more than 130 authors.

Why it matters

It shows DeepSeek is putting serious engineering effort into the infrastructure behind agentic AI, not just model releases, as it competes with far more heavily funded US labs.

The bear case

Publishing a research paper isn't the same as shipping a product; there's no indication yet that DSec powers any consumer-facing DeepSeek release.

What to watch

Whether DeepSeek ties DSec to a new agent product launch.

Steve BallmerLeadershipOtherPrivateAI read · — Fallout

Ballmer accepts NBA ban, hands Clippers to interim CEO

The move

Steve Ballmer reversed course and accepted the NBA's one-year suspension over salary-cap violations tied to Kawhi Leonard, plus a $30 million fine and forfeiture of five first-round draft picks, which the team has already paid. On September 21, the Clippers named trial lawyer and longtime season-ticket holder John S. Gibson as interim CEO and governor to run basketball and business operations during Ballmer's ban.

Why it matters

It's an unusually direct penalty against an NBA owner, and Ballmer stepping back rather than fighting it in court removes a distraction for the league even as it costs the Clippers real draft assets.

The bear case

A one-year ban from team activities doesn't touch Ballmer's ownership stake or his fortune, which is still mostly Microsoft stock, so the personal financial impact is limited.

What to watch

How Gibson handles basketball and business decisions Ballmer would otherwise have made himself.
