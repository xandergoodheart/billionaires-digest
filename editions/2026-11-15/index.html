Tesla to end Cybertruck production in 2027

The move

Musk said in a Nov. 9 X post that Tesla will stop building the Cybertruck in about eight months, after CNN reported Nov. 6 that Tesla plans to wind down production at its Austin plant. Musk said the factory space will go to the two-seat Cybercab robotaxi and that Tesla will "have to return deposits for a lot of people." Tesla doesn't disclose Cybertruck sales, but industry estimates cited by TechCrunch put total sales at about 71,000 since November 2023, short of the roughly 250,000 a year Musk once projected.

Why it matters

Tesla is reallocating a flagship but underperforming product line to robotaxi production, the business Musk has staked much of Tesla's future valuation on.

The bear case

Musk says Cybertruck isn't unprofitable, so ending it trades away existing revenue for a robotaxi ramp that is still unproven at scale.

What to watch

How many Cybertrucks Tesla builds and sells before the line shuts down, and the pace of Cybercab production that replaces it.

AMD falls 8.8% in Nvidia-linked chip selloff

The move

AMD shares fell 8.75% to close at $534.06 on Friday, Nov. 13, giving back earlier gains as a sector-wide AI chip selloff deepened, Stock Titan and The Motley Fool reported. The slide came amid continued uncertainty over Nvidia's leadership following CEO Jensen Huang's Nov. 12 resignation; the Fool said AMD was "caught in the downdraft stemming from uncertainty surrounding Nvidia."

Why it matters

Even a chipmaker with its own strong results isn't immune when doubts about the dominant AI player's leadership unsettle the whole sector.

The bear case

AMD's own business hasn't changed; the drop reflects sector sentiment rather than any new problem at the company.

What to watch

Whether chip stocks stabilize once Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter results Nov. 17.

Dell falls as AI-server selloff deepens

The move

Dell Technologies shares fell 8.16% to around $340.87 as of 10:05 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 13, GuruFocus reported, as a selloff in AI-server stocks deepened following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's Nov. 12 resignation. The report said Dell builds most of its AI servers on Nvidia hardware, tying its order outlook to confidence in Nvidia.

Why it matters

Dell's AI server business depends heavily on Nvidia chips, so doubts about Nvidia's leadership flow straight into how investors price Dell's biggest growth line, days before its next earnings report.

The bear case

Dell has already guided to about $19 billion of AI server revenue this quarter regardless of who runs Nvidia, and the stock remains up sharply for the year.

What to watch

Dell's fiscal third-quarter earnings call on Nov. 24.

Arm falls on report tied to Nvidia's CEO search

The move

Arm Holdings shares fell 4.63% to $190.53 on Friday, Nov. 13, The Motley Fool reported, as chip stocks kept reacting to Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia's CEO. The report cited the Financial Times saying Nvidia's board had approached Arm CEO Rene Haas about potentially succeeding Huang; Arm's board told CNBC it has "not formally engaged any potential candidates."

Why it matters

A move by Haas would leave SoftBank's Arm, chaired by Masayoshi Son, searching for its own new chief executive at the same time Nvidia is.

The bear case

Nothing has been confirmed about Haas leaving Arm, and the drop may simply reflect the broader chip-sector pullback rather than company-specific news.

What to watch

Any formal statement from Arm or Nvidia on Nvidia's permanent CEO search.

Broadcom falls as chip-sector selling widens

The move

Broadcom shares fell 4.76% on Friday, Nov. 13, StockTitan reported, as selling tied to Nvidia's leadership upheaval spread beyond Nvidia and AMD to Broadcom. The report gave no company-specific cause for the move.

Why it matters

Broadcom's size and AI-chip exposure make it one of the first stocks investors sell when sentiment turns on the sector, even without news of its own.

The bear case

Broadcom has had no negative company-specific news this week, and the move may reverse quickly once sentiment stabilizes.

What to watch

Broadcom's fiscal fourth-quarter and full-year results, due Dec. 9.
