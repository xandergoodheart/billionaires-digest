Nvidia posts record $115.8B quarter under interim CEO Kress

The move

Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, with Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110%. GAAP gross margin was 74.8% and diluted EPS was $2.70. For the fourth quarter, Nvidia guided to $130.0 billion in revenue, plus or minus 3%. The results, released Nov. 17, were presented by interim CEO Colette Kress, who also remains CFO; Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and a board member on Nov. 12, with board member Harvey C. Jones becoming Nvidia's first-ever board chair.

Why it matters

The blowout quarter shows Nvidia's AI chip demand hasn't slowed even as the company works through its first leadership transition in 33 years, which should calm investors who feared the earnings would be overshadowed by the CEO search.

The bear case

A single strong quarter doesn't tell investors who will run Nvidia long-term, and the company still hasn't named a permanent CEO or explained Huang's exit.

What to watch

Whether Nvidia names a permanent CEO, and Huang's expected appearance before Congress before Thanksgiving, Nov. 26.

Buffett gives away $5.5B in Berkshire stock to family foundations

The move

Warren Buffett converted 6,150 Berkshire Class A shares into 9,225,000 Class B shares and donated them on Nov. 16 to four family foundations: the Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation (3,690,000 shares) and, with 1,845,000 shares each, the Sherwood Foundation, the Howard G. Buffett Foundation and the NoVo Foundation. Based on Berkshire Class B's Nov. 13 closing price of $592.94, the gift was worth about $5.5 billion. Buffett's remaining personal stake is 182,140 Class A shares and 1,162 Class B shares, worth about $162 billion.

Why it matters

The gift continues the pattern Buffett set under his Giving Pledge of donating Berkshire stock directly to family foundations rather than cash, letting them decide how to deploy it.

The bear case

The size of future gifts depends on Berkshire's stock price, which Buffett himself has said could limit how much the foundations receive in a down year.

What to watch

Whether Buffett makes another round of foundation gifts next year on a similar schedule.

David TepperInvestmentFinanceMU · PrivateAI read · ▲ Doubling down

Tepper's Appaloosa piles into SpaceX, Micron takes top spot

The move

Appaloosa Management's Nov. 16 Form 13F for the quarter ended Sept. 30 shows a 3,883,329-share stake in SpaceX worth $556.5 million, its fourth-largest disclosed position; the prior quarter's 13F had shown a new 225,000-share SpaceX stake worth about $38.5 million. Micron Technology (1,250,000 shares, $1.33 billion) is now Appaloosa's largest holding, just ahead of Amazon (4,475,000 shares, $1.11 billion). The fund's total reported US equity value was $7.56 billion across 24 positions, down from $7.73 billion the prior quarter.

Why it matters

The sharply bigger SpaceX bet shows Tepper is willing to tie up fund capital in a company that still has no public stock, betting on a future listing or sale.

The bear case

A private holding like SpaceX can't be sold on the open market if Tepper needs to raise cash quickly, unlike his public positions.

What to watch

Whether SpaceX moves toward a public listing, which would let outside investors see a market price for stakes like Appaloosa's.

Jeff BezosInsider tradeAI & techAMZNAI read · — Tax-smart giving

Bezos gifts 771,000 Amazon shares to nonprofits

The move

A Form 4 filed Nov. 16 shows Jeff Bezos gave away 771,492 Amazon shares to nonprofit organizations in two transactions: 674,088 shares on Nov. 12 and 97,404 shares on Nov. 13. The filing lists the reason as "contributions to non-profit organizations." Bezos still holds about 878.6 million Amazon shares directly, plus 5.1 million more held through family trusts and an LLC for which he disclaims beneficial ownership.

Why it matters

Bezos has repeatedly used direct stock gifts rather than cash for his giving, which lets the receiving nonprofits sell the shares without the donor paying capital-gains tax.

The bear case

The filing doesn't name which nonprofits received the shares, so the public can't yet see where the roughly 771,000 shares actually went.

What to watch

Whether any recipient nonprofits disclose the gift themselves.

Gina RinehartInvestmentIndustrialsPrivateAI read · ▲ Big private bet

Rinehart's Hancock Prospecting reveals $1.2B SpaceX stake

The move

Hancock Prospecting's Nov. 16 Form 13F for the quarter ended Sept. 30 shows a 8,000,005-share stake in SpaceX valued at $1.21 billion, its largest disclosed US position. The filing also lists stakes in rare-earth miner MP Materials (14,861,212 shares, $702.8 million), Teck Resources (10,298,293 shares, $677.5 million) and Hudbay Minerals (15,885,729 shares, $425.9 million), plus index-fund holdings including the Invesco QQQ Trust. Total reported portfolio value was $5.75 billion across 44 holdings.

Why it matters

The filing shows Gina Rinehart, who built her fortune in iron-ore mining, extending that same resources-focused strategy into rare earths and a space company, rather than sticking only to mining stocks.

The bear case

As with other investors' SpaceX stakes, the $1.2 billion valuation is Hancock Prospecting's own mark on a private company with no public trading price to confirm it.

What to watch

Whether Hancock Prospecting's next 13F shows the SpaceX position growing further.

Carlos Slim Helu & familyInvestmentEnergyPBFAI read · — Steady holder

Carlos Slim still holds 11% of PBF Energy

The move

A Schedule 13G/A filed Nov. 16 shows Carlos Slim's investment vehicle, Control Empresarial de Capitales, holding 13,326,127 Class A shares of PBF Energy, or 11.0% of the class. The filing comes after a year of sales; Slim's PBF stake had stood near 25% at the start of 2026.

Why it matters

The filing confirms Slim remains PBF Energy's largest disclosed outside shareholder even after trimming the position through the year as the refiner's stock rallied.

The bear case

A shrinking stake over the year suggests Slim has been taking profits rather than adding to the position, which could continue.

What to watch

Whether Slim's next filing shows further sales or a stabilized stake.

Masayoshi SonInvestmentAI & techINTCAI read · — Still in

SoftBank's $10.45B Intel stake unchanged in new filing

The move

SoftBank Group's Nov. 16 Form 13F for the quarter ended Sept. 30 shows it still holds all 86,956,522 Intel shares from its $2 billion investment announced in August 2025, now valued at $10.45 billion and SoftBank's largest disclosed US stock position. The filing also lists stakes in T-Mobile US (10,000,000 shares, $1.63 billion) and Taiwan Semiconductor (565,000 shares, $257.7 million); Nvidia and Arm do not appear in the filing.

Why it matters

Holding the Intel stake steady, rather than trimming it, signals Masayoshi Son is still committed to the chipmaker's turnaround a year after taking the position.

The bear case

Intel's stock has been volatile, and a large, unhedged single-stock bet like this can swing SoftBank's reported portfolio value sharply in either direction.

What to watch

Whether SoftBank's next quarterly filing shows any change to the Intel position.

Bill GatesInvestmentHealthDNAAI read · — Quiet stake

Bill Gates's Cascade discloses 6.2% Ginkgo Bioworks stake

The move

A Schedule 13G/A filed Nov. 16 shows Cascade Investment, Bill Gates's personal investment vehicle, holding 3,322,584 Class A shares of Ginkgo Bioworks, or 6.2% of the class, based on shares outstanding as of Oct. 30.

Why it matters

The filing keeps Gates listed as a significant holder in the synthetic-biology company, an area he has backed elsewhere through the Gates Foundation's biotech investments.

The bear case

A 13G/A filing only discloses a passive stake and doesn't show whether Gates has been adding to or trimming the position recently.

What to watch

Whether Ginkgo Bioworks' own filings show other large holders changing their stakes.
