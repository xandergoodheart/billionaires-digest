How to play

  1. Draft five. Pick five different players whose salaries add up to 100 or less.
  2. Pick a captain. One of your five scores 1.5 times their points.
  3. Save before the Monday lock. Picks lock at 9:30 AM New York time, when US markets open. Then nobody can change them and everyone's picks become visible. Saving after the lock sets next week's team.
  4. Score every trading day. Weeks run Monday to Friday on US market days. Scores update once a day.

Joined late? Your first save also enters you in the current week, until Friday. It scores from the next trading day.

Scoring

  • Daily move: each pick's portfolio return that day in percent × 100, rounded. +1.23% is +123; a down day is negative.
  • Insider buy: +25 when the player files a Form 4 open-market purchase (code P) that day. The only bonus.
  • Captain: their whole day, insider buy included, × 1.5 and rounded.
  • Salaries come from each player's Public Stake Value rank and recent form. They're fixed for the week once it opens.

Show more: benchmarks
  • S&P 500 (SPY): SPY's daily return × 100, times 5 so it compares with a five-pick team (no captain).
  • Top 5 richest: the five highest-ranked draftable people, with the richest as captain. It ignores the cap.
  • Perfect team: the best possible five under the cap with the best captain, worked out after the week ends.
Show more: how a portfolio is measured
  • A portfolio is the person's current controlling stakes and holdings on their profile that trade on a US exchange, including US lines of foreign companies (ADRs) used for daily moves only.
  • When every holding has an exact disclosed share count, holdings are weighted by value (value-weighted). Otherwise the companies a person controls together count for 75% and their other stakes for 25%, with each group split by value when every holding in it has a share count and equally when not (controls-weighted, 75/25). Someone with only one group puts 100% in it. Each player card shows the method and the weights.
  • A quote that did not trade that day counts as 0%. Days with no market (weekends and holidays) do not score.
  • Private companies, funds and family holdings have no daily price, so people whose wealth is mostly private cannot be drafted.
  • Scores use public prices and disclosed holdings and are approximate.
  • Lineup projections and the matchup win chance are simple estimates from recent points. They are not forecasts.

Leagues & your account

Leagues

Show more: your account and data
  • Without an email, clearing your browser's site data or switching devices loses that account.
  • We keep only what the game needs: your nickname, coins, picks, league memberships and trades, plus your email if you add one. See the Privacy Policy and the site Terms.
Show more: name rules and fair play
  • No slurs, insults, sexual words, or names that pretend to be us or our staff. Some words are blocked automatically, and we may rename or remove anything else that breaks this rule.
  • There is no chat. Nicknames and league names are the only text players can write.
  • We may remove accounts, nicknames or leagues that abuse the game, try to break it, or use more than one account to move markets or leaderboards.

Fine print

Free, for fun, not advice

The game is free: nothing to buy, no entry fee, no prizes. Coins and points have no cash value and can't be bought, sold, cashed out, traded or sent to another player; leaderboards are for bragging rights. Built on public information about the world's richest people, its scores, prices and results are not predictions or recommendations to buy or sell anything.
