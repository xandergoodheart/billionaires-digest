The move

SoftBank completes final $10B tranche into OpenAI

The move

SoftBank completed the third and final $10 billion tranche of its $30 billion commitment to OpenAI's latest funding round on Oct. 1, deployed through SoftBank Vision Fund 2 in three installments of $10 billion each since April. SoftBank's cumulative investment in OpenAI now stands at $64.6 billion, for roughly a 13% stake. SoftBank funded part of the commitment with $11.1 billion raised last month in the largest high-yield corporate bond sale globally, and it canceled the remaining $10 billion of undrawn capacity on an earlier $40 billion bridge loan.

Why it matters

Son is finishing what he started rather than scaling back, even as he's publicly warned about AI safety risks this month — he's betting big on OpenAI specifically while urging caution on the technology generally.

The bear case

SoftBank is funding part of this with debt, and a stake in a company that doesn't trade publicly is much harder to value or exit than one of Son's listed holdings.

What to watch

Whether SoftBank discloses further follow-on commitments to OpenAI's next funding round.

SoftBank sells record $11.1 billion junk bond to fund AI bet

The move

SoftBank Group raised $11.1 billion in a dollar-and-euro junk bond sale around Sept. 24, which it called the largest high-yield corporate bond sale on record, surpassing Numericable Group's $10.9 billion issuance from 2014. The deal priced in five tranches, from a 3.5-year dollar note at 8.625% to a 7.5-year dollar note at 9.75%, plus two euro tranches at 7.125% and 8%. It follows a ¥1 trillion (about $6.3 billion) retail bond SoftBank sold earlier in September, as the company funds its roughly $64.6 billion commitment to OpenAI.

Why it matters

SoftBank has issued $14.6 billion in high-yield bonds in 2026 alone, about 63.4% of the entire Asia-Pacific and Japan junk-bond market this year, showing how much debt Masayoshi Son is taking on to fund his OpenAI bet rather than selling existing assets.

The bear case

Yields above 9% on some tranches show bond investors see real risk in lending to SoftBank for an AI bet that hasn't paid off yet; borrowing this expensive only works if OpenAI's valuation keeps climbing.

What to watch

OpenAI's progress toward an IPO, which SoftBank is counting on to justify the cost of this debt.

SoftBank sells record $11.1 billion of bonds for its OpenAI bet

The move

SoftBank is selling $11 billion of bonds this week — $10 billion in dollar notes across three maturities plus €1 billion in euro notes across two — to partially fund a follow-on OpenAI investment closing next month, on top of nearly $65 billion it has already committed to the startup. It's SoftBank's biggest junk-bond deal of the year after a string of other debt raises, including a $5 billion expansion of its Arm Holdings margin loan.

Why it matters

It shows how much Masayoshi Son is willing to borrow, and at what cost, to keep funding OpenAI: yields on the notes are among the richest SoftBank has ever paid, and its 2031 dollar bond now yields 8.2%, up from 6.7% in January.

The bear case

Piling debt on debt to fund a stake in a company that still isn't public is a bet that OpenAI's eventual valuation outruns SoftBank's rising borrowing costs.

What to watch

Whether the bond deal closes at its full size and whether OpenAI's IPO timeline slips further.
