Arnault family rebuilds LVMH's holding company to lock in control

The move

The Arnault family plans to fold its LVMH stake into Christian Dior SE, then rename that entity Agache SCA, replacing Christian Dior as the family's single listed holding vehicle over LVMH.

Why it matters

A simpler structure makes it easier to keep LVMH under family control across generations, at a time when Arnault has not named a single successor among his five children.

The bear case

Restructuring family control doesn't fix LVMH's underlying problem: luxury demand has been soft enough this year that Arnault fell out of the world's top 10 richest.

What to watch

Whether the restructuring comes with any signal on succession.
