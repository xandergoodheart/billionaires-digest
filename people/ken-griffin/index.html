Ken Griffin closes $1.1 billion Miami land deal for Carnegie Mellon

The move

Ken Griffin closed a $1.1 billion purchase of roughly 35 acres of Wynwood land from developer Moishe Mana around Sept 29-30, Florida's largest land assemblage deal on record. The site will become a Carnegie Mellon University Miami campus, part of Griffin's $3 billion gift to the university, with about $2 billion earmarked for the Miami campus. CMU plans to start enrolling students there in 2028.

Why it matters

It's one of the largest single philanthropic real estate plays by a billionaire this year, and it reshapes a well-known Miami neighborhood.

The bear case

Big land deals tied to future university campuses can slip on construction timelines and permitting well past a stated enrollment target.

What to watch

Groundbreaking on the Wynwood campus

Ken Griffin gives $3 billion to Carnegie Mellon, a record gift

The move

Citadel founder Ken Griffin pledged $3 billion to Carnegie Mellon University on Sept. 30, the largest individual gift in U.S. higher-education history, surpassing Phil and Penny Knight's $2 billion gift to Oregon Health & Science University in 2025. About $2 billion will fund a new Carnegie Mellon campus on a 35-acre site in Miami's Wynwood neighborhood, with construction starting in 2027 and students arriving in 2028. The remaining $1 billion goes to Carnegie Mellon's Pittsburgh campus, including $500 million for its School of Computer Science, which will be renamed the Kenneth C. Griffin School of Computer Science.

Why it matters

Griffin moved Citadel's headquarters from Chicago to Miami in 2022 and has become one of the city's biggest civic donors since; this gift ties a major research university's brand to the city he's betting on.

The bear case

A $2 billion campus that won't open until 2028 is a long-term bet that Miami's appeal to researchers and students holds up over several years, not a done deal.

What to watch

Whether other finance executives who moved to Miami make similarly large civic or academic donations there.
