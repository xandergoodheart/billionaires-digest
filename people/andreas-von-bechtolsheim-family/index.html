Arista's Bechtolsheim sells 260,000 shares

The move

A Nov. 9 SEC filing shows Arista Networks co-founder Andreas von Bechtolsheim sold 260,000 shares of the company in 16 separate trades on Nov. 5, at weighted-average prices ranging from $159.11 to $171.45 per transaction. The filing says the sales were made under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan he adopted on Feb. 20, 2026, through a family trust for which he is trustee. After the sales, the trust still held about 180 million Arista shares.

Why it matters

Pre-arranged trading plans like this one are routine and don't necessarily signal a change in view, but the size of the sale is a reminder of how much Arista stock is still concentrated with its founders.

The bear case

Because the sale was set up months in advance under a 10b5-1 plan, it says nothing about Bechtolsheim's current view of Arista's prospects.

What to watch

Arista's next Form 4 filings for signs the selling pace is changing.

Arista Networks posts fastest revenue growth since its IPO

The move

Arista Networks reported third-quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, above its own $3.3 billion guidance and up 58.5% year over year, with non-GAAP EPS of $1.20 versus $0.77 a year earlier and a 64% non-GAAP gross margin. The company raised its full-year 2026 revenue forecast to $13.3 billion and gave an initial 2027 target of about $18.5 billion, above the roughly $16.4 billion Wall Street had modeled. CEO Jayshree Ullal called it "another momentous quarter with the highest year-over-year revenue growth since our IPO" and said it reflects the company's "increased relevance for the generative and agentic AI era." Shares jumped after hours, with JPMorgan raising its price target to $270 from $250. Arista co-founder and largest shareholder Andreas von Bechtolsheim, through the Bechtolsheim Family Trust, holds about 14.6% of the company.

Why it matters

The networking gear that connects AI data centers together is turning out to be as sought-after as the chips inside them, and Arista's growth rate shows that demand isn't slowing.

The bear case

Arista has disappointed investors on margin or guidance details in several recent quarters despite beating estimates, so a strong headline number hasn't always translated into a lasting stock gain.

What to watch

Whether Arista's initial 2027 revenue target of about $18.5 billion holds up as the year progresses.

Bechtolsheim files to sell $45 million more Arista stock

The move

Arista Networks co-founder Andreas Bechtolsheim filed notice on Oct. 30 to sell 267,892 shares, worth about $45.2 million, under a trading plan he adopted in February 2026, according to an SEC Form 144 filing. It's one of several similarly sized sales under the same plan this year, including roughly $60 million worth of stock in both August and September.

Why it matters

The filing is a routine disclosure under a pre-set 10b5-1 trading plan, not a new decision -- but it shows one of Arista's largest holders has been steadily trimming his stake all year.

The bear case

A Form 144 is only a notice of intent to sell, not a confirmed sale, and Bechtolsheim remains one of Arista's largest shareholders.

What to watch

Arista Networks' Q3 earnings, due November 3.
