Adani pledges over ₹1 lakh crore for West Bengal by 2035

The move

Gautam Adani said his group will invest more than ₹1 lakh crore in West Bengal by 2035, covering ports, logistics, renewable energy, power, roads, green cement and hyperscale data centers. He also laid the foundation for a 2,000-bed hospital in Kolkata, part of a separate ₹4,000 crore healthcare investment.

Why it matters

The announcement came a day after the Hurun India Rich List put Adani back ahead of Mukesh Ambani as India's richest person.

The bear case

Pledges over a decade-long horizon are easy to announce; how much becomes signed contracts depends on approvals and market conditions that can shift many times before 2035.

What to watch

How much of the pledge turns into signed contracts and groundbreakings over the next year.
