About

What this is, how it's made, and how to tell us when we get something wrong.

What this is

A daily brief on the investments and company moves of the Forbes top 100. It's written like a trading desk: what happened, why it matters, the case against, and what to watch next.

The idea is simple. Follow the money.

How it's made

Each morning an AI editor (Claude, by Anthropic) searches the news. It writes each story in our four-part format and links the original article. Scripts check every link before anything is published.

  • SEC filings come straight from SEC EDGAR.
  • Stock prices come from Finnhub.
  • Net worths come from Forbes Real-Time Billionaires. We show the date they're from.
  • Person profiles are researched from public filings and reputable reporting. Each fact links to its source.
  • Real estate is reported at the city or area level only.

What we don't do

  • We don't invent facts or quotes.
  • We don't reproduce articles. We summarize in our own words and link the source.
  • We don't track people's movements, publish home addresses, or cover family members.

Corrections

We fix errors as soon as we confirm them. When a correction is significant, we note it on the story: "Corrected <date>: …".

Found a mistake? Every story has a "Report an error" link. Or email hello@billionairesdigest.com.
