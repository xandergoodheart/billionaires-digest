Privacy

Billionaires Digest is a free news site and a free-to-play fantasy game. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect when you join the waitlist or play, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist or ask for a sign-in link.
  • Your referral code and, if a friend's link brought you here, which waitlist member referred you.
  • Your captain pick and the name of any league you reserve or join.
  • Where you came from: the campaign tags in the link you used (utm source, medium and campaign) and the website that sent you, if your browser shares it.
  • Basic events: that you signed up, confirmed, picked a captain, reserved or joined a league, copied or shared an invite link, and, once we send the email brief, that it was sent and whether you clicked it.
  • If you play the online game: your nickname, your picks and your play-money results.

We do not collect payment details. The game is free, with no purchases, cash-out or prizes.

Why

  • To keep your place in line, confirm your email and let you sign back in.
  • To credit referrals, run leagues and show the game's leaderboards.
  • To send the email brief and launch news you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To count, in total, how the waitlist is doing (for example how many people confirmed). We look at totals, not at individual people.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Our email provider delivers confirmation emails and the brief.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website; fonts load from Google Fonts and the sign-in library from the jsDelivr CDN.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or ad networks on this site.
  • We don't show other players your email address. Leagues and leaderboards show names you choose.

Stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your sign-in session, your theme setting and your solo game on your device. The waitlist page also remembers, for the current visit only, the referral or invite link you arrived with until you join.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask to see or delete your data by emailing hello@billionairesdigest.com from the address you signed up with.

Contact

Questions about this page: hello@billionairesdigest.com.
