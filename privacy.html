Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Billionaires Digest ("we", "us") runs billionairesdigest.com: a free news site and a free-to-play fantasy game. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect when you play or save your team, why, and what you can do about it. It goes with our Terms.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you save your team, ask for a sign-in link, or sign up for The Billionaire Brief.
  • Whether you ticked "Also send me The Billionaire Brief". It is unticked unless you tick it.
  • Your invite code and, if a friend's invite link brought you here, which member invited you.
  • Your Season 1 captain pick and the name of any league you reserve or join.
  • Where you came from: the campaign tags in the link you used (utm source, medium and campaign) and the website that sent you, if your browser shares it.
  • Basic events: that you signed up, confirmed, picked a captain, reserved or joined a league, copied or shared an invite link, and, for emails we send, that one was sent and whether you clicked it.
  • If you play online: your guest account, nickname, picks, leagues and play-money results.

We do not collect payment details. The game is free, with no purchases, cash-out or prizes.

Why

  • To run your account: confirm your email, attach it to your team and let you sign back in.
  • To send account emails, such as confirmation and sign-in links and notices about your team or the game.
  • To send The Billionaire Brief, only if you ticked the box. Every Brief has an unsubscribe link.
  • To credit invites, run leagues and show the game's leaderboards.
  • To count, in total, how the site and game are doing (for example how many people confirmed). We look at totals, not at individual people.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Our email delivery provider sends confirmation and sign-in emails and The Billionaire Brief.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the sign-in library from the jsDelivr CDN; like any website, they receive your IP address when they send those files.

These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't track you across other websites.
  • We don't show other players your email address. Leagues and leaderboards show names you choose.

Do Not Track

Because we don't track you across websites and use no third-party trackers, our site works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, email, picks and leagues: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Basic events (the list above): up to 13 months.

Stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your sign-in session, your theme setting and your team on your device. The home page also remembers, for the current visit only, the invite link or campaign tags you arrived with until you save your team.

Age

Billionaires Digest's game and email sign-up are for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from The Billionaire Brief with the link at the bottom of any Brief.
  • Ask to see or delete your data by emailing hello@billionairesdigest.com from the address you signed up with. We delete your account and email on request.

Changes to this page

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also tell people who have saved their team by email.

Contact

Questions about this page: hello@billionairesdigest.com.
