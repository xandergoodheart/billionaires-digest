      About the Academy
      • Each school sums up a billionaire's approach in a few principles, written in our own words from their own letters, interviews and books, and from reputable reporting. Every principle links its sources.
      • Lessons explain what a real decision teaches. Facts come from the sources on each game card; the "watch out" line is the risk or counter-lesson.
      • Quiz questions only reuse the lessons and principles you have seen. No new facts.
      • Diplomas and unlocks are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
      • This is education about how people think, not advice to buy or sell anything.
